# SUPRA

> Supra is a full-stack, high-performance Layer 1 blockchain ecosystem with native oracles, verifiable randomness, automation, and cross-chain bridging. Supports both MoveVM and EVM smart contracts.

Full documentation: https://docs.supra.com/llms-full.txt
